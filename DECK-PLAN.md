## Design rules (all slides)
- Keep the rounded font + beige background.
- Green for the ONE number/word that matters per slide; everything else black.
- Number bigger than headline on Problem / Numbers / Scale.
- Same headline size + position every slide; Stash logo same corner each time.
- One card illustration max per slide; let the center breathe.
- Zero typos — proof every slide twice.

## Protect the demo
- Hero Gmail pre-connected <1hr before (on Google test-user list).
- 20–30s backup video of a clean scan, queued.
- Hands beat as insurance after Slide 3: "Phone out, search 'gift card,' hand up if you find one."
- Never fake a number on screen.

## Q&A hedges
- Small survey sample → "Small sample, but every group said the same thing, and it lines up with the national $21B."
- "Isn't it just a reminder app?" → "You can't remind yourself about money you forgot you had. We find it, then delete every excuse not to use it."
- "How do you make money?" → "An in-app store where we earn a small cut" (vision slide / Q&A only — never lead with it).
